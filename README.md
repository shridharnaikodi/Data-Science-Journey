## 🚀 Projects

### [AAL Apparel Sales Analysis — Q4 2020](Projects/AAL-Apparel-Sales-Analysis)

An end-to-end EDA project on 7,560 Australian retail transactions, built to answer a real business question: which states, demographics, and time slots drive revenue, and where should the company focus next?

- **Data Wrangling:** caught a silent whitespace bug in categorical columns that `isna()` doesn't catch but breaks `groupby()`; applied min-max normalization to Sales and Unit
- **Data Analysis:** descriptive stats, skewness check, weekly/monthly/quarterly resampling
- **Visualization:** Seaborn bar charts, heatmap, boxplots, trend lines
- **Key finding:** VIC leads all states with $105.6M in sales — ~41% ahead of #2 NSW — while demographic group and time-of-day differences are minor (within 2%), pointing to a state-footprint issue rather than a product-assortment one

**Tech stack:** Python · Pandas · NumPy · SciPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn · Jupyter Notebook
